5. EXACT MATHEMATICAL AUDIT: WHY PPL LOOKS MUCH HIGHER THAN THE LOW TRAINING LOSS NUMBERS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your instinct to question the gap between "loss = 1.45 / 1.02 / 0.25" and "PPL = 93.97 / 311.48 / 1215.13"
is completely justified. We audited every formula in `train_vault_v2_4.py` and `bench_vault_traditional.py`.
There is NO bug in the evaluator math — instead, FOUR different scales/denominators are being printed:

REASON A — Training prints 4 different loss units (3 of them are NOT unmasked nats/token!):
  1) `BPB = 1.4554` is BITS-PER-BYTE, NOT cross-entropy nats/token!
     If `1.4554` were nats/token, PPL would be `exp(1.4554) = 4.29`.
     In reality:
       NLL_nats_per_token = BPB * ln(2) * (UTF8_bytes_per_token)
     - On `Holdout English` (6 dense CS paragraphs, 5.621 bytes/tok, 60-tok cold start):
       NLL/tok = 1.4554 * 0.69315 * 5.6208 = 5.670 nats/tok  ==>  exp(5.670) = 290.13 TokPPL!
     - On `WikiText-2` (4.244 bytes/tok):
       NLL/tok = 1.5447 * 0.69315 * 4.244  = 4.543 nats/tok  ==>  exp(4.543) =  93.97 NativeTok_PPL!
  2) `ni_w = 0.253` (`exp(0.253) = 1.29` PPL) is MASKED ONLY to the 4-digit NIAH code (`CHK-3213`).
  3) `sk = 1.020` (`exp(1.020) = 2.77` PPL) is MASKED ONLY to structured ChatML code/math answers.
  4) `tr_p = 3.18 - 3.81` nats/tok (val `pr_ce = 3.285` nats/tok) IS the true unmasked prose loss!
     And `exp(3.285) = 26.72`, which matches `pr_PPL = 26.72` to the second decimal place.

REASON B — In-Domain Textbook Prose (`26.72` TokPPL) vs Out-of-Domain Raw WikiText-2 (`93.97` TokPPL):
  - `26.72` TokPPL = `3.285 nats/tok` (in-distribution textbook/STEM prose).
  - `93.97` TokPPL = `4.543 nats/tok` (`wikitext-2-raw-v1`), which is `+1.26 nats/tok` higher because:
    (i)  `wikitext-2-raw-v1` uses Penn-Treebank detached punctuation (`@-@`, `@,@`, `@.@`, `= = Header = =`)
         that NEVER appears in VAULT's clean textbook/code corpus (every `@-@` spikes 8-12 nats).
    (ii) Total token budget: VAULT has trained on `0.575B` tokens total (heavily STEM + code + NIAH),
         whereas Mamba-1/2 trained on `300B`, Mamba-3 on `100B`, and SmolLM on `600B` (`170x-1,000x` more
         general web/encyclopedia facts, proper nouns, dates, history/sports/pop-culture).

REASON C — Why `WikiText-2 Word_PPL = 311.48` is 3.3x `NativeTok_PPL = 93.97` (EXPONENTIATION):
  - SmolLM's BPE tokenizer splits `241,218` WikiText-2 whitespace words into `304,837` BPE tokens
    (`1.2638` tokens per word).
  - Dividing total NLL by words instead of tokens happens INSIDE `exp(...)`, which raises PPL to a POWER:
      Word_PPL = exp(NLL / words) = (NativeTok_PPL)^(tokens / words) = (93.97)^(1.2638) = 311.48
  - Even SmolLM-135M (600B tok) jumps from `17.26` NativeTok_PPL to `36.60` Word_PPL (`17.26^1.33`).

REASON D — Why `LAMBADA TargetWord_PPL = 1215.13` is over 1,000:
  - LAMBADA does NOT compute perplexity over the passage. It sums log-prob across ALL subwords of ONLY the
    final word of 1,500 novel/fiction passages (`mean_final_word_nll = 7.103 nats` across ~2.5 subwords
    ==> `exp(7.103) = 1215.13`, `acc = 12.27%`). Predicting unseen fiction character names / final nouns
    zero-shot requires broad web/book pretraining scale (`100B+` tokens).
